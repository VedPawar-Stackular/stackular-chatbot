The same team that builds your product helps you run it — post-launch ownership is not optional, it's part of how we work. ## How We Build Products
Our product development process is structured in four phases:
- **Discovery:** We align on the problem, user needs, and business goals before writing a line of code. This includes competitive analysis, user research, and a clear product roadmap.
- **Prototype & Validate:** We build lightweight prototypes to validate assumptions with real users early, avoiding costly rework later.
- **Build:** We use an agile, sprint-based delivery model. AI-assisted development accelerates implementation — progress is measured in working software, not slide decks.
- **Launch & Own:** We deploy, monitor, and iterate. The same engineers who built it stay accountable for its performance post-launch.

## Technology We Use
We are technology-agnostic and select the stack that fits the problem. Common choices include:
- **Frontend:** React, Next.js, Angular, React Native (mobile)
- **Backend:** Node.js, Python (FastAPI, Django), .NET
- **Cloud:** AWS, Azure, Google Cloud — all three with equal depth
- **AI/ML:** Python-based ML pipelines, LLM APIs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq), custom model fine-tuning
- **Databases:** PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Cosmos DB, Snowflake, Redis
- **DevOps:** Terraform, GitHub Actions, Azure DevOps, Docker, Kubernetes

## What Makes Us Different
We are a referral-only firm — every engagement starts with trust. We don't hand off to a delivery team after the sale; the people who scoped the work are the same people building it. Our AI-first mindset means we evaluate AI acceleration at every phase, not as an afterthought. Our fastest delivery on record is 10 days.

# Engagement Models & How We Work
> [Source: https://www.stackular.com/]
> [Category: Engagement]

## How Engagements Start
Stackular is a referral-only business — we don't have a traditional sales team. New clients come through introductions from existing clients and trusted networks. If you've been referred to us, the engagement typically begins with a discovery conversation to understand your problem before any proposal is made.

## Types of Engagements
We work in three main models depending on the nature of the work:
- **Project-based (fixed scope):** A defined deliverable, timeline, and budget. Best for well-scoped initiatives like app builds, platform migrations, or design sprints.
- **Time & Materials (T&M):** Flexible engagements billed on hours or sprints. Best for ongoing product development, managed services, and iterative roadmaps.
- **Managed Services / Retainer:** Stackular takes ongoing ownership of a platform, cloud environment, or data infrastructure. We monitor, maintain, optimize, and support it continuously.

## Typical Project Timelines
Timelines vary by scope, but as reference points:
- Fastest delivery on record: **10 days**
- Typical discovery and prototyping phase: **2–4 weeks**
- Full product build (MVP): **8–16 weeks** depending on complexity
- Cloud migrations and platform modernizations: **3–6 months** for mid-size enterprises
- Managed services engagements are ongoing with no fixed end date

## Pricing
We tailor pricing to the engagement model and scope. We do not publish standard rate cards. The best way to get a sense of investment for your project is to [reach out directly](https://www.stackular.com/contact-us) — we'll have a scoping conversation before any numbers are discussed.

# AI Strategy at Stackular
> [Source: https://www.stackular.com/]
> [Category: AI Strategy]

## Our AI Philosophy
AI is not a separate service line at Stackular — it's a delivery principle. Every engagement is evaluated for where AI can accelerate delivery, reduce manual work, or improve outcomes for the end user. We call this an AI-first mindset.

## What AI-First Means in Practice
- **In product development:** We use AI-assisted code generation and testing to compress timelines. Features that used to take 6 weeks can often be delivered in 2–3.
- **In enterprise systems:** We embed Agentic AI into CRM workflows, service desks, and AP processes to automate routine tasks and reduce human error.
- **In data intelligence:** We apply machine learning for predictive analytics, anomaly detection, and intelligent dashboarding.
- **In cloud operations:** AI-driven monitoring and alerting to detect and resolve incidents faster, often before they impact users.

## Agentic AI
Agentic AI refers to AI systems that take autonomous, multi-step actions — not just responding to a single prompt but completing workflows end-to-end. Stackular has implemented Agentic AI in production across document processing (invoice automation), case management, and extranet platforms. This is a core differentiator in our Enterprise Systems and Product Development practices.

## Responsible AI
We are deliberate about where AI is applied. For regulated industries like healthcare and government, we ensure AI decisions are explainable, auditable, and compliant with relevant frameworks. We do not deploy AI where it creates unacceptable risk without appropriate human oversight.
